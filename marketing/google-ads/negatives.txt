# ============================================================================
# MATCH-TYPE DECISIONS (not removals - every term above is from the brief)
# ============================================================================
# "the voice" is PHRASE, not broad. A broad negative "the voice" blocks any
#   query containing both "the" and "voice" in any order - which would kill
#   "who is the best voice teacher in los angeles" and "the voice lessons near
#   me". Phrase keeps the TV show out and the customers in.
# "how to sing", "learn to sing free", "american idol", "piano tuning" and
#   "guitar repair" are PHRASE for the same reason: as broad negatives their
#   individual words ("sing", "voice", "piano", "guitar") appear in queries we
#   are paying for.
# Everything in groups 1 and 3 is safe as broad - none of those words appear in
#   a query we want.
#
# ============================================================================
# THREE TERMS FROM THE BRIEF THAT WILL COST YOU TRAFFIC - REVIEW IN WEEK 2
# ============================================================================

# the first weekly review and narrow if the report supports it.

#   Also blocks "free vocal assessment los angeles" and "free consultation
#   singing lessons" - i.e. people searching for the exact offer being sold.
